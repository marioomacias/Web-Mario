Esmark Finch Vehicle Engineering

After listening to Rolando's advice to build a portfolio, and everything Juan taught me along the way, I put together this space to document my Esmark Trial where I have been able to put hands on experience at vehicleengineering.ie, where I got to meet the team, see the facilities, and work with real enterprise infrastructure for the first time.

The trial covered two distinct roles, back to back — here's what each one involved:

IT Support & Infrastructure Technician

17 May – 23 Aug
  • Remote assistance via RustDesk and Lenovo endpoint resets for digital kiosk deployments
  • User provisioning, account creation & credential security in Microsoft 365 Business, aligned with IAM protocols
  • Crimping, testing and terminating Cat5e/Cat6 cabling, patch panel installs, and LTE/WiFi/GPS cabling on R1900 mobile routers
  • Frontline helpdesk support — guided Logistics through a printer language error and general hardware/software troubleshooting

Vehicle Graphics Installer / Fitter

24 Aug – 14 Sep
  • Precision physical installations and hardware integrations on fleet vehicles in a high-demand engineering environment
  • Graphics preparation and vehicle wrapping operations, moving from screen-based work to hands-on fitting
  • Applied strict quality control, spatial accuracy, and structured organization to every asset modification

Acknowledgments

A special thank you to Juan and the entire Esmark team for their time, patience, and for sharing their knowledge and facilities with me during this trial. It has been an invaluable learning experience that has solidified my determination to build a career in this industry.

I think that's genuinely the point of life — stepping outside your comfort zone, learning, and pushing forward to get a little better every day. This portfolio is my professional space, but it's a mindset I try to carry everywhere.

If you're looking for someone who shows up ready to learn, ask questions, and put in the work — I'd welcome the chance to prove it.
