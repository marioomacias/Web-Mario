Esmark Finch Vehicle Engineering

After listening to Rolando's advice to build a portfolio, and everything Juan taught me along the way, I put together this space to document my Esmark Trial where I have been able to put hands on experience at vehicleengineering.ie, where I got to meet the team, see the facilities, and work with real enterprise infrastructure for the first time.

During the trial days I got a first look at how things really work from the inside. Here's what I picked up along the way:

Hands on & Executed:

  • Remote assistance via RustDesk
  • User account & password creation in Microsoft 365 Business
  • LTE, WiFi & GPS cabling on an R1900 antenna
  • Lenovo endpoint reset for a digital Kiosk demo
  • CMD, Node.js/npm, and HTML/CSS/JS fundamentals in VS Code

Observed & Supported:

What the trial taught me

Beyond the tools and software, these days were my first real look at how a technology company with big ambitions runs day to day:

  • Seeing, for the first time, how work really gets done in a tech company with big goals.
  • Learning to document my work, ask the right questions, and take suggestions on board.
  • Stepping outside my comfort zone to look for something new — a mindset, not just a skill.
