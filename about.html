> Éire

A year and a half ago I made the boldest call of my life: I packed a bag and moved from Spain to Ireland completely on my own — no safety net, no contacts, no plan B.

Starting from zero taught me more about resilience than any manual could. I learned a new language, built a routine, and earned my independence early. I chose to put down roots in Dublin because it's one of Europe's real tech hubs — the kind of place where the people around you push you to get better, not just get by.

> Marathons_and_The_Long_Way_Round

Technical skills can be taught. What can't be taught is showing up — for the early run, the long shift, the problem that doesn't get solved on the first try. Endurance sport is where I built that habit.

  • Consistency: long-term goals are won through daily, unglamorous repetition — training plans and ticket queues aren't so different.
  • Resilience: pushing through the wall at kilometre 30 builds the same mental toughness I rely on troubleshooting critical infrastructure under pressure.
  • Curiosity: every long run doubles as an excuse to explore — a new route, a new trail, a corner of Ireland I hadn't seen yet. Right now that curiosity is aimed at a 46km trail, because the flat road stopped being enough.
